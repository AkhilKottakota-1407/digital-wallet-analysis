# digital-wallet-analysis
# 💳 Digital Wallet Customer Analysis (EDA Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of a digital wallet dataset to understand customer behavior, spending patterns, and engagement trends.

The main objective is to extract meaningful insights that can help fintech companies:
- Understand customer usage patterns  
- Identify high-engagement user segments  
- Improve customer experience strategies  

---

## 🎯 Objective
- Analyze customer transaction behavior  
- Study trends across demographics (age, income, location)  
- Identify factors influencing **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**  
- Generate business insights using visualizations  

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset consists of **7000 customers** with demographic, transactional, and engagement-related features.

### 🔑 Features Included
- Customer demographics (Age, Location, Income Level)  
- Transaction details (Total Transactions, Avg Value, Total Spent)  
- Engagement metrics (App Usage, Active Days)  
- Rewards & benefits (Cashback, Loyalty Points)  
- Support & feedback (Tickets, Satisfaction Score)  
- Target variable: **LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)**  

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### 💰 Spending Trends
- Customers aged **40–60 spend the most**  
- **Middle-income users** contribute slightly higher spending  
- Spending is consistent across locations  

---

### 👥 Customer Distribution
- Equal distribution across **Urban, Suburban, Rural**  
- Majority users fall in **20–60 age group**  
- Middle-income group has the highest representation  

---

### 💳 Transaction Patterns
- Higher transaction values for **middle-income customers**  
- Most active users belong to **20–60 age range**  
- Slightly higher transaction activity in **suburban areas**  

---

### 🎁 Cashback Analysis
- Higher cashback observed for:
  - Urban users  
  - UPI payment method  
  - Middle-income group  
- Cashback is higher in **20–60 age group**  

---

### 📈 LTV Analysis
- Higher LTV observed for:
  - Middle-income customers  
  - Age group **20–40**  
  - Credit card users  
  - Suburban users  

---

### 🎯 Loyalty Points Insights
- Higher for:
  - UPI users  
  - Urban customers  
  - Middle-income group  
- No strong relation with app usage frequency  

---

### 📣 Referral Trends
- No major variation across categories  
- Higher referrals in **20–60 age group**  

---

### 🛠 Support Tickets
- More tickets raised by:
  - Active users (**20–60 age group**)  
  - Urban users  
  - UPI / Wallet users  

---

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction
- Most ratings fall between **5–6**  
- No major variation across payment methods, location, or age  

---

## 📊 Correlation Insights
- Strong positive relationship:
  - **Total Spent → LTV**  

- Key takeaway:
  - Customers who spend more tend to have higher lifetime value  

- Negative correlation:
  - **Loyalty Points vs Total Spent**  

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- High-value customers are typically:
  - Middle-income  
  - Aged **20–40**  
  - Highly active users  

- Engagement (transactions, usage) plays a major role in LTV  

- UPI users show strong activity in:
  - Cashback  
  - Loyalty points
